Questions parents ask

What is Picture Sudoku?

Picture Sudoku works like regular Sudoku but uses pictures instead of numbers. Kids place pictures in a grid so that each row, column and box has every picture exactly once. It builds logic and problem-solving skills before children are confident with numbers.

What is the difference between the printable and the paperback?

The printable PDF has 55 puzzles across 4 difficulty levels (Easy 4×4, Medium 6×6, Hard 6×6, Very Hard 9×9) for ages 4 to 10. You download and print it at home. The paperback has 33 easy and medium cut-and-paste puzzles for ages 4 to 6 and is shipped by Amazon.

Can I print the PDF more than once?

Yes. Once you purchase the digital download you can print as many copies as you need for personal or classroom use.

What age is Picture Sudoku for?

The printable version covers ages 4 to 10 with puzzles from easy (4×4) to very hard (9×9). The paperback is best for ages 4 to 6 with easy and medium puzzles only.

Does my child need scissors?

Only for the paperback. It is a cut and paste book: children cut out the pictures and paste them into the grid, which is great for fine motor skills. The printable PDF does not require cutting.
