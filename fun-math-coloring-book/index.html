Questions parents ask

What age is Fun Math Coloring for?

Ages 4 to 8, from preschool to first grade. It works best when your child can count to 10 and is starting to add and take away.

How does color by sum and difference work?

Every shape in the picture has a small math problem, like 2 + 2. Your child solves it, finds the answer in the crayon key at the top (for example 4 = yellow) and colors the shape with that crayon. When the answers are right, the hidden picture appears, so the page checks itself.

What is inside the book?

29 color by number pictures (animals, vehicles, sea life, treats and more) with addition and subtraction within 10, a crayon key at the top of every page. It is a paperback printed and shipped by Amazon.
