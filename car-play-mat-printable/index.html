What's on the play mat

A two-way road runs down the middle of the mat, with colorful parking spots on both sides and a train track along the edge. Each parking spot shows a number from 1 to 8 together with the number word, like "5 Five", so children see the digit and the word side by side.

How to play

  1. Start easy: let your child drive a car into any spot and say the number out loud together.
  2. Call out a number and ask your child to park a car in that spot.
  3. Put a small sticker with a number on each car and ask your child to match every car to its spot.
  4. Count the parked cars together, then drive them away one by one and count down.
  5. For older preschoolers, ask questions like: which spot comes after 4? Which number is the biggest?

What children practice

  • Recognizing the numbers 1 to 8 and their written words
  • Counting and one-to-one matching
  • Hand control as they steer cars into the spots
  • Taking turns and new words when siblings or friends play together

Printing tips
